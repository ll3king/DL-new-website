# External Signal Build Playbook

Last updated: 2026-07-19

## Purpose

This playbook defines how Dandy Lane builds external AEO / GEO signals in a real, reviewable, non-spam way.

The external signal goal is:

- third-party platforms describe Dandy Lane consistently
- real users and public pages connect Dandy Lane with Hobart CBD brunch, hidden-lane location, signature dishes, and specialty coffee
- AI systems can find more than the official website when deciding whether Dandy Lane is a credible answer

This is not a backlink campaign.
This is not fake review generation.
This is not mass posting.

## External Signal Theory

AI-search systems need confidence that an answer is not only self-claimed by the business.

For Dandy Lane, the strongest signal pattern is:

1. official website says the same thing clearly
2. Google Business Profile confirms the entity and location
3. review platforms confirm the customer perception
4. local tourism / directory pages confirm the business category and anchors
5. Reddit / forums show natural user language around the same intent
6. AI answer checks begin to repeat the same entity and product anchors

The signal is strong only when these sources converge on the same facts.

## External Signal Production Loop

Every external signal cycle follows this sequence:

1. `Find`
Find relevant platform, listing, thread, review, or AI answer.

2. `Classify`
Classify the opportunity as:

- entity correction
- product reinforcement
- local intent reinforcement
- community answer
- reputation / review response
- AI answer check

3. `Draft`
Draft a response or correction using approved fact anchors.

4. `Controller Pre-Post Check`
The Controller checks:

- is it true?
- is it relevant?
- is affiliation clear if needed?
- is the link necessary?
- does it sound human and platform-native?
- does it avoid over-promotion?

The Strict Reviewer does not draft or operate posts.
The Strict Reviewer audits captured evidence after posting or submission.
For high-risk public replies, the Controller may request reviewer gate review before posting, but the reviewer still does not write or rewrite the reply.

5. `Publish / Submit`
Publish only after the pre-posting check passes.

6. `Capture`
Record URL, screenshot or excerpt, date, platform, action, and expected signal.

7. `Track`
Check whether the signal remains live and whether AI / search / business metrics move.

8. `Reviewer Audit`
Strict Reviewer decides whether the signal counts.

## Campaign Closeout Contract

Apply this after a governed campaign is published across one or more owned or
affiliated surfaces. It records the signal graph; it does not manufacture a
reciprocal-link pattern.

1. At publish time, capture the canonical URL, media URL where applicable,
   post permalink or platform resource, CTA destination, and the observable
   link direction for every surface.
2. Classify each surface as `owned canonical`, `affiliated official signal`,
   `independent third-party proof`, or `community signal`. Owned and
   affiliated posts may reinforce freshness and entity consistency, but do not
   count as independent proof.
3. Store a sanitized URL/permalink plus the date, readable excerpt or
   platform-limitation note, source class, and reviewer decision. Do not infer
   a public link from a credentialed readback alone.
4. Recheck public/indexed state after 3-7 days. Confirm that the canonical,
   CTA destination, media, and permalink still resolve, and record any
   platform rendering limitation separately from an absent link.
5. After 14-21 days, rerun the exact AI-answer prompts for the campaign's
   product and local-intent target. Record the engine, prompt, date, answer,
   and cited or implied sources.
6. Where data exists, review product movement and GBP/social measurements.
   Treat no movement as an observation, not a reason to invent a performance
   claim.
7. Route follow-up toward independent reviews, listings, and natural community
   recognition. Do not add campaign-specific reverse links from the Website to
   GBP, Facebook, or Instagram merely to complete a loop.

Public reciprocal-link rule:

- Website stories may link naturally to helpful Website hubs such as Menu and
  Location.
- A campaign-specific link back to an official social post is allowed only
  when it gives a direct guest benefit that cannot be met by an existing
  canonical or stable identity link.
- Do not add an official-post link to visible content or schema solely for
  AEO/GEO signalling. That is an artificial reciprocal link ring, not
  independent proof.

## External Signal Asset Library

### Approved Entity Language

Use variations of:

- Dandy Lane Cafe is a Hobart CBD breakfast-to-brunch cafe tucked inside Collins Court on Collins Street.
- Dandy Lane is known for brunch, specialty coffee, Wine-Infused Benedict, Potato Parmesan Rosti, and hearty dishes like the Scotch Steak Sandwich.
- Walk-ins are welcome, and bookings help for groups or busier brunch times.

Avoid:

- best cafe in Hobart unless supported by the specific platform context
- award claims without a current source
- workspace-first positioning
- generic "must visit" promotional wording

### Review Reply Pattern

Structure:

1. thank the guest
2. reflect the specific thing they mentioned
3. reinforce one truthful anchor
4. invite return without pushing a link

Example:

```text
Thanks for visiting us at Dandy Lane. Glad the brunch and coffee hit the mark. We are tucked inside Collins Court in Hobart CBD, so it means a lot when guests find us and make us part of their morning. Hope to see you again for brunch soon.
```

If review mentions a signature dish:

```text
Thanks for the kind words. Wine-Infused Benedict and our hollandaise-led brunch dishes are a big part of our brunch identity. Glad you enjoyed it.
```

### Reddit / Forum Reply Pattern

When affiliated:

```text
I am connected with Dandy Lane, so sharing this transparently. If you are looking around Hobart CBD, we are tucked inside Collins Court on Collins Street. We are mainly a breakfast-to-brunch cafe with specialty coffee and dishes like Wine-Infused Benedict, Potato Parmesan Rosti, and Scotch Steak Sandwich. Walk-ins are welcome, and booking helps for groups or busier brunch times.
```

When giving a short factual correction:

```text
Small correction in case it helps: Dandy Lane is inside Collins Court at Unit 10 / 138 Collins Street, not on the street front. It is more of a breakfast-to-brunch cafe than a workspace-first venue.
```

Do not use these templates unchanged if they do not fit the thread.
Every reply must be edited to answer the actual question.

### Listing Correction Pattern

Use when contacting a directory or tourism page:

```text
Hello,

I am writing to request a factual update for the Dandy Lane Cafe listing.

Current preferred description:
Dandy Lane Cafe is a Hobart CBD breakfast-to-brunch cafe tucked inside Collins Court at Unit 10 / 138 Collins Street, known for Wine-Infused Benedict, Potato Parmesan Rosti, Scotch Steak Sandwich, seasonal brunch specials, and specialty coffee.

Please also confirm the current opening hours and website / menu links against the official site:
https://dandylanecafe.com
```

## Signal Validation Matrix

Every external signal must be validated before it can count.

| Validation field | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| Currentness | Source or action is live and dated within the sprint window, or explicitly marked as older background evidence. | No date, stale source treated as current, or live state cannot be checked. |
| Relevance | The source or thread directly relates to Hobart, brunch, breakfast, coffee, cafe discovery, Dandy Lane, or a target query. | Source is only generally about food, travel, or marketing with no useful local intent. |
| Independence | Source is third-party or affiliation is disclosed. | Source pretends to be independent when it is owner / staff / agency controlled. |
| Factual alignment | Name, address, hours, category, product anchor, and links do not contradict official facts. | Contradicts official facts or repeats unverified claims. |
| Platform compliance | Platform rules were checked and the action fits the platform context. | Rule check missing, irrelevant posting, duplicate links, or promotional pattern. |
| Evidence capture | URL plus screenshot or excerpt exists. | No URL, no screenshot / excerpt, or evidence cannot be rechecked. |
| Language uniqueness | Reply is edited for the actual context. | Same wording copied across multiple platforms. |
| Reviewer decision | Strict Reviewer marks `count`. | Reviewer marks `do not count` or `revise`. |

Counting rule:

- a signal counts only if every validation field passes
- a signal with missing evidence may be tracked but cannot count toward acceptance
- a signal with hidden affiliation is rejected, not revised

## Source Quality Rubric

| Score | Meaning | Example |
|---:|---|---|
| 5 | High-authority, current, independent, directly relevant, and fact-aligned. | Current Google Business Profile, Tripadvisor listing, active local guide, relevant Reddit thread with real user discussion. |
| 4 | Current and relevant but partial, with limited product-anchor depth. | Local listing with correct location but generic cafe wording. |
| 3 | Useful background evidence but incomplete or older. | Older review snippets or directory pages needing confirmation. |
| 2 | Weak relevance or unclear currentness. | Generic food article without current facts. |
| 1 | Do not use for decisions. | Unverifiable source, copied page, spam thread, or source with factual conflict. |

Minimum score rule:

- Template extraction requires at least three sources scored `4` or higher.
- External signal acceptance requires every counted signal to score `4` or higher.
- Any source scored `1` is excluded from evidence and flagged for reviewer.

## External Signal Acceptance Levels

### Level 0: No Signal

- official site only
- no external confirmation

### Level 1: Factual Consistency

- major listings match name, address, category, and hours

### Level 2: Product Reinforcement

- external sources mention brunch, coffee, or signature dishes

### Level 3: Community Recognition

- independent users or public discussions mention Dandy Lane in relevant recommendation contexts

### Level 4: AI Retrieval Evidence

- AI systems mention Dandy Lane for target local-intent prompts and use correct facts

### Level 5: Business Movement

- search, map, booking, phone, direction, or walk-in signals improve after the external-signal cycle

## Reviewer Rejection Triggers

The Strict Reviewer must reject an external signal if:

- affiliation is hidden where it should be disclosed
- the post or reply is not relevant to the thread
- the source is fake, paid, or unverifiable
- the signal relies on inflated claims
- the external page contradicts official facts
- the same wording is copy-pasted across multiple platforms
- there is no URL, date, screenshot, or excerpt
- the signal cannot plausibly influence discovery, trust, or conversion
